## Brand & Style
This design system crafts a personal portfolio experience for 鍾沛良 (CHUNG PEI-LIANG) rooted in the classic golden-era cel animation and painted background tradition. The aesthetic channels Studio Ghibli’s evocative pastoralism: sun-drenched rolling meadows, hand-mixed watercolor skies, hand-inked line work, and the palpable warmth of tactile artist parchment.

The emotional core is nostalgic, contemplative, and artisanal. It invites visitors into a tangible sketchbook world rather than a sterile digital feed. Visual tension is achieved by balancing delicate, organic watercolor washes with decisive cel-shaded contours inked in charcoal—giving structural authority to an otherwise ethereal, dreamlike environment.

## Colors
The palette evokes traditional animated film backgrounds painted on textured rag paper.

- **Primary (`#1E2B24` - Deep Twilight Forest Green):** Serves as the anchor color for dominant brand elements, solid display containers, and footer baselines, evoking ancient cedar canopies.
- **Secondary (`#4A90E2` - Bright Sky Blue):** Used for lively interactive accents, focal links, and clear-weather highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F28C52` - Sunset Warm Orange):** Provides warm, narrative energy for badges, prominent call-to-actions, and project milestones.
- **Neutral (`#2C2A29` - Dark Charcoal Ink Line):** Acts as the foundational contour line, border tone, and primary body text tone, replacing artificial blacks with a hand-ground sumi ink quality.
- **Atmospheric Pigments:**
  - **Canvas Base (`#FBF8F1` - Warm Parchment):** The foundation substrate across light mode viewports.
  - **Watercolor Wash (`#E8F1F5` - Soft Mist Blue):** Tonal card surfaces and atmospheric background blocks.
  - **Flora (`#68B984` - Meadow Grass Green):** Success indicators, category chips, and organic graphic details.
  - **Illumination (`#F5C75B` - Sunflower Yellow):** Hover states, stellar accents, and editorial stamps.

## Typography
The typography marries the literary elegance of traditional book printing with the gentle legibility of animated film titles.

- **Headlines (Literata):** A bespoke storybook serif with organic, humanistic cuts and generous x-height. It communicates personal storytelling, scholarly depth, and classic craftsmanship across project titles and personal chapter headings.
- **Body & Labels (Nunito Sans):** A friendly, subtly rounded grotesque that mimics the soft ink bleed of hand-printed typography without sacrificing technical legibility. The rounded terminals mirror the hand-painted contours used across card frames and button outlines.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-yet-composed 12-column grid system reminiscent of animation layout boards and framing sheets:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `3rem` canvas margins, keeping narrative blocks comfortably framed like watercolor vignettes.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Complex editorial layouts collapse into single-column story sequences.

Generous vertical breathing space between sections (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) emulates the pacing of quiet cinematic beats ("ma" - the space between moments), avoiding aggressive commercial density.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is rendered through cel-shaded animation principles and physical watercolor layers rather than synthetic multi-layered blur shadows:

1. **Ink-Line Hard Shadows (Cel-Shaded Elevation):** Interactive elements drop flat, solid-color offset shadows (no blur, 2px to 4px horizontal and vertical offset) tinted in `neutral` (`#2C2A29` at 15% to 30% opacity) or `primary` (`#1E2B24`), mimicking cel layers resting slightly above painted backdrops.
2. **Wash Layering:** Base layouts rest on `#FBF8F1`. Hovered or elevated containers transition their background to tinted washes like `#E8F1F5` (Mist Blue) or `#F5C75B` (Sunflower Warmth at 20% opacity).
3. **Paper Substrates:** Cards feature soft paper-grain microtextures via overlay noise blends (opacity 2-4%), grounding UI elements into tangible paper pulp.

## Shapes
Geometry is gently organic and distinctly hand-drawn.

- **Base Radius:** Elements use `roundedness: 2` (base 0.5rem, with larger components utilizing 1rem to 1.5rem).
- **Organic Variance:** Major containers, case study previews, and media viewports subtly employ asymmetrical corner radii (e.g., `16px 14px 18px 12px` or CSS clip-paths) to evoke paper cutouts and naturally cut celluloid sheets.
- **Inking Strokes:** Borders maintain a continuous 1.5px to 2px solid stroke in `#2C2A29` (Charcoal Ink), grounding shapes with the deliberate precision of a master keyframe artist.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#F28C52` (Sunset Warm Orange) or `#1E2B24` (Deep Twilight Green) with `#FBF8F1` text. Framed with a 1.5px `#2C2A29` charcoal border. Drops a 3px 3px 0px `#2C2A29` cel shadow. On hover, translates -1px, -1px with an expanded 4px 4px shadow.
- **Secondary:** Filled in `#E8F1F5` (Mist Blue) or pure `#FBF8F1` with `#1E2B24` text, bound by a 1.5px ink outline and a subtle flat 2px offset shadow.
- **Text/Ghost:** Charcoal ink text with an animated watercolor brush underline on hover, tinted in `#68B984` or `#4A90E2`.

### Cards & Project Folios
- Built with a `#FBF8F1` parchment fill, surrounded by a 1.5px `#2C2A29` contour.
- Images inside cards are masked with soft rounded corners and capped by a delicate inner paper stroke.
- Interactive states reveal a gentle watercolor tint bleed of `#E8F1F5` and lift slightly with an ink-line cel drop shadow.

### Chips & Tags
- Used for disciplines, software competencies, and project categories.
- Rendered in pill or gentle rectangular forms with 1px ink borders.
- Backgrounds use muted nature washes: `#68B984` (meadow), `#F5C75B` (sunflower), or `#E8F1F5` (mist), set at 25% opacity with dark charcoal typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls use the `#FBF8F1` parchment background framed by a 1.5px charcoal line.
- Focus states do not use sterile digital glow halos; instead, they shift to a vibrant 2px `#4A90E2` sky blue outline accompanied by a flat 2px offset shadow.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons resemble stamped ink marks with solid charcoal checkmarks and fills.

### Lists & Timelines
- Experience timelines and project breakdowns use dotted or dashed hand-inked vertical threads.
- Milestone nodes are represented by organic circular beads filled with `#F5C75B` or `#F28C52` bordered in charcoal ink.

### Narrative Vignettes & Chapter Dividers
- Section dividers use soft, horizontal organic brush strokes or small nature-inspired dingbats (leaves, cloud wisps, or lantern motifs) styled in charcoal ink rather than straight geometric rules.
